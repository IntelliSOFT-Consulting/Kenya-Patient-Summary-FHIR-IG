### Glossary

<table border="1" cellpadding="8" cellspacing="0" class="dataframe table table-striped table-bordered">
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Term</th>
      <th>Definition</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Business process</td>
      <td>A set of related activities or tasks performed together to achieve the objectives of the health programme area, such as registration, counselling and referrals.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Data dictionary</td>
      <td>A centralized repository of information about the data elements that contains their definition, relationships, origin, use and type of data. For this DAK, the data dictionary is provided as a spreadsheet.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Data element</td>
      <td>A unit of data that has specific and precise meaning.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Decision-support logic</td>
      <td>A set of decision rules for standard and exceptional cases that is separate from the business process.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Decision-support (for health workers)</td>
      <td>Digitized job aids that combine an individual’s health information with the health worker’s knowledge and clinical protocols to assist in diagnosis and treatment decisions.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Decision-support table</td>
      <td>Semi-structured way to depict each discrete decision to be embedded in the system.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Digital health</td>
      <td>The systematic application of ICT, computer science, and data to support informed decision-making and improve health and wellness.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Digital tracking</td>
      <td>Use of a digitized record to capture and store clients’ health information for follow-up and service tracking.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Functional requirement</td>
      <td>Capabilities the system must have to meet user needs and achieve tasks in the business process.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Health information system</td>
      <td>A system for collecting, processing, reporting, and using health information to improve service effectiveness and efficiency.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Health management information system (HMIS)</td>
      <td>An information system designed to assist in the management and planning of health programmes.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Interoperability</td>
      <td>The ability of different applications to access, exchange, and use data in a coordinated and timely way across boundaries.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Non-functional requirement</td>
      <td>General attributes and features ensuring usability and addressing technical constraints, e.g., offline support or password protection.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Persona</td>
      <td>A generic description of a person involved in or benefitting from a health programme.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Person-centred point of service system (PCPOSS)</td>
      <td>Digital systems that support health service delivery at the point of care, including features like EMRs and decision-support tools.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Standard</td>
      <td>A published specification used to ensure consistent, uniform information sharing in digital applications.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Task</td>
      <td>A specific action in a business process.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Terminologies</td>
      <td>Structured vocabularies used in clinical care to consistently represent health-related concepts.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Workflow</td>
      <td>A visual representation of the logical flow of tasks, events, and decision points in a business process.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
